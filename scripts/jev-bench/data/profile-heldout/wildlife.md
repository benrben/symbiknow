# Wetland bird surveys and observation records

## Bird monitoring

Survey the same shoreline route at dawn to reduce differences between visits. Count each flock once and note birds that depart before the next observation point. Use distant landmarks to distinguish overlapping groups. Record unusual behaviour and avoid entering nesting areas during the breeding season.

## Field data collection

Write the date, time, weather, water level, and observer names on every survey sheet. Record uncertain species separately until photographs are reviewed. Preserve the original counts when corrections are made. Enter the sheets into the seasonal register and flag visits with incomplete route coverage.
