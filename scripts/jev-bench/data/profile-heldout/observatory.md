# Telescope alignment and instrument maintenance

## Telescope alignment

Align the mount with the celestial pole before calibrating its pointing model. Centre several reference stars on both sides of the meridian and record the remaining offsets. Check the alignment after the telescope has been moved. Verify tracking on a fixed target before starting long exposures.

## Optical maintenance

Inspect optical surfaces under diffuse light before deciding to clean them. Remove loose dust with a blower and avoid rubbing a dry surface. Keep covers in place when the instrument is idle. Check cable strain and power connectors during the monthly inspection, and document every replaced component.
