# Digitising photographs and preserving master files

## Photo digitisation

Scan photographs at a resolution that preserves fine detail without adding artificial sharpening. Include a colour target at the start of each batch. Clean the scanner bed between items and inspect the captured image for dust or clipped edges. Retain the capture settings alongside the accession number.

## Digital preservation

Store untouched master images separately from edited access copies. Compute checksums at ingestion and compare them during scheduled audits. Maintain copies at two independent locations. Record every format conversion and retain the original file when a new access format is produced.
