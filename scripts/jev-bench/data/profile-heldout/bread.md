# Maintaining starter cultures and fermenting dough

## Sourdough starters

Feed an established starter with weighed flour and water at a consistent temperature. Mark the starting level in the jar and observe the rise before the next feeding. A culture that repeatedly collapses before feeding needs a shorter interval. Keep clean utensils and discard cultures with visible mould.

## Bread fermentation

Fold the mixed dough during the first part of bulk fermentation to strengthen its structure. Watch for gas pockets and a gradual increase in volume rather than relying only on a fixed timer. Shape the dough gently, then allow the final proof to proceed until its surface slowly rebounds after a light press.
