# Orchard pruning and irrigation

## Tree pruning

Remove crossing branches while trees are dormant. Keep the central canopy open enough for sunlight to reach the fruiting wood. Cut damaged limbs above healthy buds and disinfect tools between trees. Record which branches were removed so the next inspection can distinguish fresh growth from damaged tissue.

## Drip irrigation

Run drip lines along the root zone. Inspect emitters weekly for blocked outlets and leaks. Check moisture below the mulch before extending the watering cycle. Newly planted trees need shorter intervals than established trees, and heavy rain should postpone the next cycle.

## Calendar note

Send purchased replacement emitters to the office for Bookkeeping after the field inspection.
