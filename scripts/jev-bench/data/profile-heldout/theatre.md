# Stage rigging and emergency procedures

## Stage rigging

Check rated loads before hanging lights or scenery above performers. Inspect slings for fraying and confirm that every shackle pin is secured. Use a second person to verify attachment points. Keep the area below a suspended load clear until the load is settled and its controls are locked.

## Emergency evacuation

Walk every exit route before an audience enters. Ensure illuminated signs are visible from each seating section. Assign staff to guide visitors and agree on the announcement used during an emergency. Practise clearing the stage and auditorium without carrying equipment into narrow exit passages.
