# Woodworking joints and workshop tool care

## Woodworking joinery

Mark the shoulder lines from one reference face before cutting a mortise and tenon. Fit the tenon gradually so the shoulders meet without twisting the assembly. Dry-fit the pieces before applying adhesive. Check the diagonal measurements of a frame while the clamps are still loose enough to adjust.

## Tool maintenance

Remove resin deposits from saw blades and inspect teeth for damage. Sharpen chisels before their edges become visibly rounded. Keep handles firmly attached and store cutting edges where they cannot strike one another. Disconnect powered tools before adjusting guards or changing blades.
