# Rain garden design and runoff maintenance

## Rain gardens

Select a shallow planted depression below the contributing roof or paved area. Use soil that absorbs water while retaining enough moisture for native plants. Choose species that tolerate alternating wet and dry conditions. Keep an overflow route so exceptional rainfall does not flood nearby buildings.

## Stormwater drainage

Check inlet grates after storms and remove sediment before it blocks the flow. Inspect channels for erosion and monitor where standing water remains after a day. Keep debris out of the overflow structure. Compare the observed water path with the original grading plan before changing any channel.
