# Coastal navigation and boat safety

## Coastal navigation

Plot the departure point, offshore hazards, and arrival bearing before leaving harbour. Compare charted depths with the predicted tide. Take bearings on two fixed landmarks to confirm the plotted position. Record course changes and estimate the arrival time after accounting for currents.

## Boat safety

Inspect lifejackets and check that their straps fit each passenger. Test the bilge pump and emergency radio before departure. Keep a recovery line accessible from the cockpit. Brief passengers on the location of the fire extinguisher and agree on the procedure for a person falling overboard.
